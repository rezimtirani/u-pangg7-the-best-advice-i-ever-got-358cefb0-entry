One sentence about the best advice the writer ever received — from a grandfather who mended shoes for forty years, on why a sole wears out from the inside first.

> My grandfather, who mended shoes for forty years, told me a sole always wears out from the inside first — so fix the lining before you blame the leather.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3 standard library only) writes the sentence to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`, checked first against the competition gate: 1 to 200 characters of UTF-8 text, exactly one sentence, about the best advice the writer ever received.

```sh
python3 main.py
```
